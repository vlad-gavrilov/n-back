# Player guide

## Set up a round

Choose Visual to match positions or Dual to match both positions and spoken letters. Set N from 1 to 9, choose a field from 2×2 to 6×6 and pick a trial interval from 1 to 6 seconds. With live feedback off, the game confirms each response with **Registered** and shows the score at the end. For the scoring formula and how the game recommends the next level, see [Exercise parameters and research](research.md).

On desktop, settings sit to the left of the field. Open **Session settings** beside the heading on screens up to 864 px wide or in landscape windows up to 560 px tall. During a round, progress and **Stop** appear above the field. Response buttons become available after warm-up. They sit below the field in portrait and on either side when a short landscape window has enough room.

## Keyboard and touch

In Visual, press **Space** for a position match. In Dual, press **F** for a position match, **J** for a letter match, or both. On touchscreens, use the **Position** and **Audio** buttons.

Shortcuts work while the round is active. On a Russian keyboard, F and J are the physical keys marked А and О. If the browser omits the physical key code, the game recognizes the corresponding Latin and Cyrillic letters.

Space on a focused link still answers a Visual trial. Enter follows the link. On a focused response button, Space or Enter records the answer on keydown. Other buttons keep their usual keyboard actions. Held keys, typing in editable controls and input in open dialogs do not record answers. In forced-colors mode, the field and tutorial markers use the system palette.

## Sound and interruptions

In Dual settings, **Test sound** plays one letter. A success message appears when playback finishes. If the sample fails, **Try again** replays it with settings still open. You can also switch to Visual. If you close mobile settings while the sample is loading, the test continues and its status moves to the main screen. Retrying a failed round start closes settings and prepares that round.

Switching away from the page, rotating the screen or a timing interruption ends the round without a score. Losing sound also ends a Dual round. The interruption card explains what happened. Choose **Start fresh** when you are ready for a new round.

After a completed round, **Adjust session** opens settings on any screen. The result stays available until a game parameter changes. Result actions are disabled while the next round is being prepared. Open dialogs hold the background in place and restore the previous scroll position and focus when closed. Clicking the panel or its padding keeps the dialog open.

## Language and theme

Choose **EN** or **RU** in the desktop header or in a dialog. On mobile, open **Session settings** to reach the language control. The first visit uses English. Language changes preserve the settings, tutorial step and results. Language controls are disabled during audio preparation and active rounds. Interface text and accessibility labels follow the selected language. Spoken letters stay in English, and Dual uses the F/J shortcuts in both languages.

The trainer runs without an account or backend and collects no analytics or round history. The theme defaults to the current system preference on each visit until you choose a theme manually. The app saves that choice and the interface language in browser storage. If storage is unavailable, the next visit starts in English with the system theme.
