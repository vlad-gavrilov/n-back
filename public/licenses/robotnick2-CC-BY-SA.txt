The eight spoken letters (B, F, H, J, M, Q, R and W) are excerpts from
"English alphabet" by Robotnick2, recorded on 19 October 2010.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:English_alphabet.ogg

The source recording and the adapted WAV files are licensed under Creative
Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC BY-SA 3.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

For N-back, the recording was resampled to 22050 Hz, cut into individual
letter names, faded for 8 ms at both edges, normalized for consistent
volume and encoded as mono 16-bit PCM WAV.
